SillyJo Privacy Policy

Last updated November 12, 2026

SillyJo is an Android entertainment app published by benahm. It includes Surprise Photo, Selfie Call, Living Painting, and Wide Awake. This policy explains how SillyJo uses the data needed for these features.

Overview

SillyJo processes data on your device. The developer does not collect personal data through the app. SillyJo requires no account and has no internet permission, ads, analytics, or third-party crash reporting. You can choose to save a Living Painting video to your gallery or share it with another app.

Camera and microphone

Surprise Photo and Selfie Call use the camera to display a live preview. They do not take, save, or send photos or videos. Living Painting records video of the person whose mouth or eyes appear in the artwork. Android asks for camera permission the first time it is needed. You can revoke it in your device settings.

Living Painting uses the microphone only when you record without music and grant microphone permission. If you deny it, the recording is silent. When you choose music, SillyJo uses that track and does not request or record microphone audio. A video you choose to share may include its recorded audio.

Living Painting videos

During recording, SillyJo writes the video to its private temporary storage. After previewing the video, you can choose what to do with it.

  • Save to gallery. SillyJo copies the video to Movies/SillyJo on your device and deletes the temporary copy after the save succeeds. On Android 9 and earlier, Android asks for storage permission when you choose to save.
  • Share. The Android share sheet lets you choose another app. SillyJo grants that app access to the video. The recipient app handles the file under its own privacy policy.
  • Redo or discard. SillyJo deletes the temporary video. If you leave a temporary recording without making a choice, SillyJo deletes it the next time you open Living Painting.

Photos and music you choose

SillyJo uses Android’s file pickers to open the image or audio file you select. It does not browse the rest of your photos or files.

  • Surprise Photo displays the image you select during the prank without copying it.
  • Selfie Call saves a private copy of the photo you choose for the fictional contact, along with its display name, so you can reuse it.
  • Living Painting displays your chosen photo without copying or retaining it. It reads your chosen audio to add it to the video and does not keep a separate copy of the track.

The artwork and music supplied with the app are public-domain works included in SillyJo.

Settings, calls, and contacts

Wide Awake saves your iris color, size, spacing, and skin tone settings on your device. Its animated eyes are drawn by the app. The feature uses neither the camera nor the microphone. SillyJo also stores your choice of app language.

Selfie Call simulates calls inside SillyJo. It does not place or receive real calls or access your contacts or call history. It can vibrate and play your phone’s ringtone according to your sound settings.

Android backup

If Android backup is enabled, Android may include local settings and the fictional contact’s display name in the backup for your Google account. That backup excludes the contact photo and temporary videos. On Android 12 and later, the contact photo may be copied during a direct transfer to another device. Google manages Android backup according to your settings. The developer cannot access the backup.

Deleting your data

You can replace the fictional contact’s name and change or remove its photo in the app. Delete saved videos from your gallery. To remove SillyJo’s local app data, go to Settings › Apps › SillyJo › Storage › Clear data, or uninstall the app.

Children

SillyJo is intended for people aged 13 and older. The app does not collect children’s personal data.

Changes
